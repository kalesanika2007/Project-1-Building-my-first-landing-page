Four habits, not forty features.

Clarity does a few things well instead of everything half-heartedly.

Focus sessions
Pick a task, set a length, and Clarity silences notifications, hides other tabs, and tracks the block automatically — so "focus time" is a real, measurable thing, not a hope.
Smart time blocks
Drop tasks into your day and Clarity arranges them around your existing meetings and your actual energy patterns, instead of leaving you to Tetris your own calendar.
Weekly rhythm
Every Friday, see which blocks you kept and which you skipped — not as a guilt trip, but as a pattern you can actually plan around next week.
Gentle nudges
One quiet reminder when a session starts, one when it ends. No streaks to protect, no red badges piling up while you're trying to concentrate.
